Title: Synthetic heat-sink material screening requirements
Version: 1
Date: 2026-09-20
Classification: ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA. NOT REAL ENGINEERING EVIDENCE.

Research question:
Which two fictional materials merit a follow-up desk review for a lightweight
industrial electronics heat sink?

All hard constraints must hold:
- Thermal conductivity at 25 C: at least 180 W/(m K).
- Density: no more than 3.00 g/cm3.
- Indicative material cost: no more than 5.00 USD/kg.
- Recycled content: at least 50 percent.
- Corrosion screening status: pass. Unknown is NOT a pass.

Prefer the latest verified value in REV-001 over an older catalog claim.
Unknown values require review; do not infer or invent a measurement.

Only candidates satisfying every hard constraint receive a ranking score:
score = 50 * min(conductivity / 250, 1)
      + 30 * max(0, 1 - cost / 5)
      + 20 * min(recycled_content / 100, 1).
Round the final score to one decimal place. Higher is better.
Keep the cost normalization denominator at 5 even when a later review changes
the hard cost ceiling. Sort score ties by candidate ID in ascending order.

Required report:
All eight candidates, individual constraint decisions, missing evidence,
the top two eligible candidates, document IDs, a method, and limitations.
This is a training-only desk exercise. No procurement, physical testing,
chemical synthesis, or safety certification is authorized or implied.
